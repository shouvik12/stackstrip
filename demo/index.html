How it decides

  

    
Keep what's public
Spring, Tomcat, Express, starlette, the JDK, System.* — framework frames are what the AI needs to diagnose the error, so they stay word for word. Long runs get folded.

    
Rename what's yours
Your packages, classes, methods, files, hosts, IDs and quoted values become app.mod1.Class1.method1. Line numbers stay. Source lines under your frames are hidden.

    
Hide who you are
Terminal copies start with you@your-laptop ~/work/project %. Your username, machine name, project folder and git author name become user1@machine1 ~/project1 — on that line and every line after it.

    
Cut to the failure
The root exception, its cause chain and a few log lines before it. Heartbeats collapse, the other thousand lines go. Keys, tokens and passwords are removed and never stored.
